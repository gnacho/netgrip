# ADR template

## Status

proposed | accepted | deprecated | superseded by ADR-NNN

## Context

What situation forces a decision. What constraints are real, which are preferences.

## Decision

The decision, stated so a future reader can tell whether a change they are
proposing matches it or breaks it.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table, and why it lost. One line each.

## Consequences

What this decision makes easy, and what it makes hard. What it rules out.
